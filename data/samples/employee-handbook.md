# Aurora Tech Employee Handbook

Aurora Tech, Lda. is a fictional software company created for the Lumen demo. This handbook covers the
first weeks at the company and the benefits available to every employee.

## Welcome

Aurora Tech builds accounting software for small businesses. The company was founded in 2019 in Braga,
Portugal, and has around 85 employees across engineering, product, sales and support.

## Your first week

### Day one

On your first day you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy at the Braga office at 9:30. IT gives
you your laptop and accounts, and you complete the security training before lunch.

### Onboarding buddy

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for the first 90 days. The
buddy answers informal questions and meets you for coffee at least once a week.

### Probation period

The probation period is 90 days for most roles and 180 days for management roles. Halfway through, you
have a check-in conversation with your manager.

## Working hours and remote work

The standard working week is 40 hours, from Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Remote
work follows the internal telework regulation: a hybrid model with at least 2 office days per week,
Tuesday being the shared office day, and at most 3 remote days per week.

## Time off

### Vacation

Employees have 25 working days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests must be submitted in the
HR portal at least 30 days in advance for periods longer than 5 days.

### Birthday leave

Every employee gets their birthday off. If the birthday falls on a weekend or holiday, the day off can
be taken on the following working day.

### Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the same day. A medical certificate is needed for
absences longer than 3 days.

## Benefits

### Health insurance

Aurora Tech provides health insurance for all employees from the first day, including dental care.
Family members can be added at a discounted rate.

### Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,000 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused
budget does not carry over to the next year.

### Meal allowance

The meal allowance is 10.20 euros per working day, paid on a meal card.

## Equipment

Employees receive a laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset. Equipment must be
returned on the last working day. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the security team within
24 hours.
